ActiveRings card

The real card bundle with invented sample data; none of it is from a real person. Each frame is a mock Home Assistant dashboard in a different theme or width. Hover a bar for its number, use the small switches on each chart to change its period, and hover a ring's row to read what fills it.

Focus activity (an option of the integration)

A ring is full when its goal is reached, so a full ring is always good news. Today: steps, exercise minutes and active energy since midnight. Last 7 days looks back over a rolling week, and each of its rows shows the seven days as seven small columns, today last: exercise minutes (goal 150, the World Health Organization's guideline), active days (the days that reached the daily exercise goal, goal 5) and the days with your focus activity (goal 2). The focus activity is an option of the integration: strength training unless you choose another, such as swimming or cycling. The number in the middle is the mean of the three rings, each counted to 100 at most. Goals are options of the integration.
